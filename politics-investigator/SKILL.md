# politics-investigator

Build durable, truth-seeking investigative dossiers from institutional roots through present developments.

## When to use

Use this skill for political, legal, economic, security, institutional, or public-affairs investigations that require official records and primary-source verification.

## Instructions

1. Establish an affair-specific slug and scaffold a persistent Markdown vault.
2. Separate verified findings, leads, and open questions.
3. Reconcile public claims and secondary summaries against primary evidence.
4. Record a governing thesis, its confidence, and evidence that could falsify it.
5. Maintain source-audit, stakeholder, follow-up, and thesis-evolution records.
